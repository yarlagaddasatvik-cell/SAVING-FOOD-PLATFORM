Food Safety & Redistribution Standards

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) based on FSSAI & Global Food Safety Guidelines for surplus food redistribution.

Thermal Management Zone

  • Hot Cooked Foods: Must be maintained at >65°C in insulated containers until distribution.
  • Cold / Perishable Foods: Must remain chilled between 2°C to 5°C.
  • The 4-Hour Rule: Cooked food held between 5°C - 65°C must be consumed within 4 hours of preparation.

Packaging & Hygiene Protocols

  • Only untouched, surplus batch food is accepted (no plate leftovers).
  • Food-grade stainless steel or recyclable leakproof containers must be used.
  • Vegetarian and non-vegetarian batches must be strictly packed and transported separately.
